Wyoming license checklist

Every job above needs a Wyoming certificate from the Professional Teaching Standards Board (PTSB), in hand or in process. A Colorado license does not transfer automatically, but for a traditionally trained teacher with a Colorado license and passed Praxis the review is routine. Time is the issue, not approval.

  1. Create a PTSB portal account, start an out-of-state application

    You are out-of-state unless your degree is from the University of Wyoming. PTSB portal. Rules on the out-of-state page.

  2. Order official transcripts sent directly to PTSB

    From your degree-granting school. They must come from the institution.

  3. Get two blue FBI fingerprint cards rolled

    Cheyenne PD or the Laramie County Sheriff will do it. About $50 processing.

  4. Upload your Colorado license and Praxis or PLACE scores

    Wyoming accepts the tests you took for Colorado. No Wyoming Praxis needed.

  5. Clear the U.S. and Wyoming Constitution requirement

    Short exam PTSB points you to, or coursework on your transcript. Ask PTSB which applies.

  6. Pay the invoice and wait

    Published out-of-state fee is $225 for a five-year license. PTSB emails the invoice after review. Four to six weeks once complete. In by Thanksgiving means licensed by early January.

PTSB is in Cheyenne: 2001 Capitol Ave, Emerson Building Room 128, 307-777-7291. If the full license drags, a substitute permit is faster and gets you working.

Have these ready as PDFs

SchoolSpring will not submit until every required item is attached. Each file a PDF under 10 MB with a one-word filename.

  • Resume with the Windsor school and grade levels
  • Copy of Colorado teaching license
  • Unofficial transcripts (official ones go to PTSB separately)
  • Praxis or PLACE score reports
  • Three professional references with phone and email
  • Two or three recommendation letters, a principal's carries the most weight
  • Cover letter you can tailor per school
  • Most recent evaluation, if you are comfortable sharing it
  • Screenshot of the PTSB portal showing the application in progress
